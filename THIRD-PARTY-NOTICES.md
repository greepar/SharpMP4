# Audio codec provenance

ManagedAacEncoder's FFT MDCT construction is adapted from glint (https://github.com/CrispStrobe/glint), pinned at 77738f3ed9b15f627196cc5bbd7f6406814ba2fb. The AAC Huffman and scalefactor-band tables are normative ISO/IEC 13818-7 / 14496-3 data cross-checked by glint's generator. Quantization, coding, PCM mixing and integration are implemented in C# here.
